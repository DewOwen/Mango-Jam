🔬 Why These Ingredients?

🥭 Mango

Provides the main flavour, aroma, natural colour and fruit pulp that makes the jam unmistakably mango.

🍚 Sugar

Adds sweetness, helps the jam become thicker, and contributes to its keeping quality.

🧪 Citric Acid

Adds a slight acidity and helps create the acidic environment needed for good jam texture and preservation.
